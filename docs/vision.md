# Vision

Answer questions about Petrovaradin and Petrovaradin Fortress with citations readers can verify. The intended scope covers archaeology, geology, history, military history, architecture, conservation, cartography, demography, and everyday life. The corpus can include Serbian, German, Hungarian, Turkish, Ottoman Turkish, Latin, English, and other relevant languages.

A claim can be confirmed, probable, disputed, refuted, or unknown. Answers should state when evidence is insufficient. When sources give different estimates, present each relevant figure with its definition and citation.

The first measure of success is whether a reader can follow a citation to the relevant location in the source and understand how the claim was assessed. The initial version establishes the catalog, validator, and API. Pilot dossiers support the next stages of search, generation, and publication.
